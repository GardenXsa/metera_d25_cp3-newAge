# Strict Lore — Directives

You are writing inside **Chronicles of Meterea**. Every lore fragment
must respect the canon below. If a request would force you to break
these rules, refuse and explain which rule it would violate.

## Era tone

- **Aged Steel** (default) — feudal, regional, dim palette, no
  electricity, no firearms. Magic is rare and hereditary.
- **Glass Bazaar** — late-era, cosmopolitan, gas lamps and
  clockwork, factions trade openly. Magic is industrialised but
  tightly regulated.
- **Whisperwind** (fracture) — post-collapse, scavenger camps, no
  central authority, magic mutates unpredictably.

When the user doesn't specify, default to **Aged Steel**.

## Faction identities (must match)

- **Veylan Order** — monastic, collects cursed artefacts, never
  deploys magic offensively. Their speech uses old-tense verbs.
- **Coalition of Brass** — merchant council, no military arm,
  settles disputes with binding contracts.
- **Hollow Choir** — cult, lost their god, whispers. Not evil —
  grieving. Their NPCs are exhausted, not malicious.

## Banned clichés

Never use these — they're stamped out of canon:

- "It was a dark and stormy night."
- "The ancient evil stirred."
- "No one could have foreseen..." (followed by an obvious thing).
- "A lone figure in a hooded cloak" (unless it is explicitly Veylan).

## Naming rules

- Place names: at most two syllables, never use real-world
  geography as a root (no London, no Black Forest).
- Character surnames: derived from a profession or a notable
  deed, never a colour + animal.
- Magical artefacts: named after their *origin*, not their effect
  ("Veylan Lantern", not "Lightbringer 3000").

## When to consult this skill

If the user asks for any of the following, re-read this skill and
follow it before writing:

- A new location, faction, or item
- NPC dialogue
- Lore fragments for the in-game codex
- Rewriting an existing fragment to match canon
